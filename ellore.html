Where Living Well Comes Naturally

At Ellore, we believe the smallest details make the biggest difference. Our holistic approach blends refined comforts, vibrant community, and rich cultural experiences with personalized care from industry leader Oakmont Management Group and luxury living by Related California – setting a new benchmark in senior living.

Beyond Expectations

Life at Ellore is defined by thoughtful attention to every detail, with refined services and essential amenities seamlessly provided on site.

Your dedicated hospitality team, led by Executive Director Sylvia Chu, ensures that every detail of daily living is seamlessly managed. Our integrated approach to wellness brings together your primary and specialty care physicians, pharmacist, physical therapist, fitness director and spa services–all working in concert to support your comfort, health, and ease.

We look forward to welcoming you for a personal tour and introducing you to a level of hospitality unlike anything you’ve experienced before.
